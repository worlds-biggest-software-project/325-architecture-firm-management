## Relevant Industry Standards or Protocols

- **AIA Contract Documents (B-Series)** — standard owner-architect agreement forms that define fee structures (hourly, lump sum, percentage of construction cost); practice management software must support AIA-aligned invoicing formats
- **AIA Architecture Billings Index (ABI) / Deltek** — monthly industry benchmark tracking billing activity; relevant for demand forecasting within firm management tools
- **AIA MasterSpec** — standard specification writing system integrated with Deltek; referenced in project deliverable management
- **Earned Value Management (EVM)** — project control methodology that compares budgeted cost of work scheduled against actual costs; increasingly expected by sophisticated clients and government contracts
- **AECO Industry BIM Standards (ISO 19650)** — Building Information Modelling standards affecting project deliverable formats and coordination workflows that practice management tools must accommodate
- **FAR / DCAA Compliance** — federal acquisition regulation accounting requirements for firms with government contracts, requiring compliant indirect cost rate structures in the accounting module

## Market Research

**Market Size:** The broader professional services automation (PSA) market for A/E firms is a segment within the global project management software market, valued at approximately USD 9.6–11.3 billion in 2026 and growing at 13–15% CAGR. Architecture-specific tooling represents a significant minority share dominated by Deltek.

**Funding:** Deltek (owned by Thoma Bravo) is the enterprise incumbent. BQE Software is bootstrapped/PE-backed with a strong mid-market position. Monograph raised Series B funding and continues targeting independent practices. Factor A/E is an early-stage entrant with venture backing.

**Pricing Landscape:** Enterprise platforms (Deltek) are quote-based with implementations commonly costing $50,000+ in year one. Mid-market platforms (BQE CORE, Productive) run $30–$70/user/month. Purpose-built lighter tools (Monograph, Factor) start at $15–$30/user/month.

**Key Buyer Personas:** Small independent architecture firms (1–10 staff, need simple time/billing), mid-size practices with multiple project managers (need portfolio visibility and phase-based fee management), large multi-office firms (need full accounting integration and government contract compliance), design-build hybrid firms (need construction phase tracking alongside design phases).

**Notable Trends:** The ABI index approached growth territory in March 2026 after 25 consecutive months of contraction, signalling improving conditions. Firm leaders cite profitability improvement and appropriate fee negotiation as top concerns for 2026. Cloud-based tools are gaining rapidly at the expense of Deltek's legacy on-premise deployments. Integration between practice management and BIM/design tools (Revit, ArchiCAD) is an emerging demand.

## AI-Native Opportunity

- **AI-assisted fee estimation** that analyses historical project data — scope, typology, client complexity, regulatory jurisdiction — to produce evidence-based fee proposals and flag scope items commonly missed in initial negotiations
- **Automated time capture** using calendar analysis, email threads, and design file activity to draft timesheet entries for architect review, addressing the chronic under-recording problem in project-based billing
- **Predictive project health monitoring** that detects early warning signals of budget overrun or schedule slip using phase completion rates and burn velocity, enabling project managers to act before problems compound
- **AI-generated client progress reports** that pull schedule status, deliverable completion, and budget consumption from the project database and draft formatted client communication with minimal human input
- **Regulatory and code intelligence** integrated into project setup that surfaces relevant building codes, zoning restrictions, and approval timelines for a project's jurisdiction, reducing research overhead early in the design phase
